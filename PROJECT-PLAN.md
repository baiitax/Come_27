## Verification & Credibility Framework
### Political Claim Classification:
- VERIFIED: Supported by official documentation
- REPORTED: Reported by credible media
- CAMPAIGN CLAIM: Provided by campaign
- PROPOSED: Future policy
- UNDER REVIEW: Awaiting verification

### Every statistic must display:
- Source: [source]
- Publication: [year]
- Last Updated: [date]

### Evidence Badges:
- VERIFIED / OFFICIAL RECORD / CAMPAIGN DOCUMENT / MEDIA REPORT / UNDER REVIEW

### Content Workflow:
DRAFT → RESEARCH REVIEW → FACT CHECK → EDITORIAL APPROVAL → CANDIDATE/CAMPAIGN APPROVAL → PUBLISH

## Verification Requirements
- All dates and descriptions must be verified against primary documents
- No unverified campaign claims as established facts
- No fabrication of achievements, statistics, quotes
- No deepfakes or deceptive testimonials
- No fake endorsements or polls
- Private voter information must not be exposed
- Aggregated public data only for dashboards

## Accessibility
- WCAG 2.2 AA compliance
- High contrast mode
- Keyboard navigation
- Screen reader labels
- Focus states
- Reduced-motion mode
- Glassmorphism must never compromise readability

## Motion Design Principles
- Subtle cinematic motion only
- No excessive bouncing, flashing, spinning logos
- No aggressive parallax
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion
- Hero: slow atmospheric movement
- Glass cards: fade + translate 20px
- Timeline: progressive reveal
- Statistics: counter animation
- Map: smooth hover
- Policy cards: soft lift
- Navigation: blur transition

## Key Design Prohibitions
- No generic campaign-poster aesthetics
- No excessive gradients
- No cartoon icons
- No oversized flag graphics
- No generic stock photographs
- No excessive green backgrounds
- No cluttered layouts
- No flashing animations
- No political clichés
- No excessive portraits
- No low-quality photographs
- No visually aggressive propaganda
- No red-heavy color schemes
- No fabricating historical evidence
- No AI-generated imagery as documentary evidence
- No unmoderated supporter content as official communication
